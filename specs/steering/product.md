# Product

Getnet merchants ask questions in a chat: about Getnet products, about their own money and machines, and about general topics such as the weather. The service answers with facts it can show, or a person takes over.

Users: small and medium merchants in Brazil. Most write in Portuguese, some in English.

Values, in order:

1. Never show one merchant's data to another.
2. Never invent a fact. Every fact comes from a tool, and a knowledge-base answer cites its source.
3. Never leave the merchant without help: if the system fails, open a ticket for a person, whose
   answer comes back into the same conversation.
4. Keep secrets out: card numbers, CPF, CNPJ and passwords never reach a model, the database or the
   logs.
5. Cheap to run: a low-cost model, local embeddings, free public APIs and free checks first.
